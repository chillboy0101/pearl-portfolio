Accepted upstream · August 2026

A fix in the Linux kernel

The mute LED on HP Laptop 15-dy0xxx machines never lit because the model was missing from the sound driver's quirk table. I traced the LED to a codec register with hda-verb, verified the fix on real hardware, and submitted the patch. The Linux sound maintainer applied it.

Read the patch Maintainer's reply 
Subject: [PATCH] ALSA: hda/realtek: Enable mute LED on HP Laptop 15-dy0xxx

The LED is controlled by COEF index 0x07, bit 0.
This was verified on the hardware with hda-verb.

Tested on an HP Laptop 15-dy0xxx
(SKU 7FU54UA#ABA, board 864F, BIOS F.40).

Signed-off-by: Carl Quist
Applied now.
Takashi Iwai, Linux sound subsystem maintainer
