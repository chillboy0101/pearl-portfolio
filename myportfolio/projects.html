gra.gov.ghPublic

GRA tax calculators

VAT, PIT and PAYE calculators on the official GRA website, built as WordPress plugins and maintained in three repositories. All three apply the 2026 rates and run entirely in the visitor's browser, so no income figures are stored.

  • VAT adds or splits out NHIL (2.5%), the GETFund Levy (2.5%) and VAT (15%) under Act 1151.
  • PIT deducts SSNIT at 5.5% and applies the 2026 resident bands under Act 1178.
  • PAYE applies the 2026 bands with GRA's personal reliefs.

  • JavaScript

Mute LED fix for HP Laptop 15-dy0xxx

The mute LED on this HP laptop (Realtek ALC236) never lit because the model had no quirk entry. I traced the LED to COEF index 0x07, bit 0, with hda-verb, tested on the hardware, and sent the fix. ALSA maintainer Takashi Iwai applied it in August 2026.
